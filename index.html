About SUMBE FARM

SUMBE FARM is a farm-direct herbal products business located in Chas, Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra. We cultivate and process premium quality Lemongrass, Tulsi and Moringa with a focus on freshness, purity and sustainability.

Our mission is to provide natural herbal products directly from farm to consumer while supporting healthy living.
